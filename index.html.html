About the College

A teacher-training institute on the NH-105 corridor in Muzaffarpur district.

Who we are

Primary Teachers Education College (PTEC), Pokhraira is a teacher education institute in the Saraiya area of Muzaffarpur, Bihar. Its focus is the D.El.Ed programme, which qualifies candidates to teach in elementary schools.

The college is recognised by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), the national body that sets and monitors standards for teacher training. NCTE’s records list its D.El.Ed recognition from the 2002–2003 session, and its institute ID is NCTE1329074.

Admission to the college runs through Bihar’s common D.El.Ed counselling, where it appears among the Muzaffarpur colleges with a seat list of 250.

Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed)

The D.El.Ed is a two-year, full-time diploma approved by NCTE. In Bihar it is the standard route into elementary school teaching, and it combines classroom study with supervised practice in schools.

Across the two years, D.El.Ed programmes generally cover the areas below. The exact syllabus and papers are set by the state, so please check the current curriculum notice.

Admissions

Seats are filled through Bihar’s state-level D.El.Ed process, not directly by the college.

How the process works

  1. Notification & registrationThe state publishes the notice and opens online registration, usually early in the year.
  2. Entrance examinationRegistered candidates sit the entrance test, and results follow some weeks later.
  3. CounsellingCandidates choose colleges by rank and seat availability. Later rounds fill vacant seats.
  4. Verification & feePresent your documents at the allotted college and pay the prescribed fee to confirm the seat.

Documents usually needed

Class 10 mark sheetClass 10 certificateClass 12 mark sheetClass 12 certificateCharacter certificate

The counselling notice may ask for more, such as category or identity documents. Keep originals and photocopies ready.

Eligibility

Candidates generally need to have passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination. Minimum marks and any relaxation for reserved categories are set each session, so please read the current notice.

Common questions

Can I apply to the college directly?

For D.El.Ed in Bihar, seats are allotted through state counselling. You register for the state process and then choose colleges, including PTEC Pokhraira, when counselling opens.

When do admissions open?

Recent sessions have opened registration in the first months of the year, with the entrance test and results a few weeks apart. Dates change each year.

What if seats remain after counselling?

The state has held additional spot rounds for vacant seats in past years. Watch the official notices for those dates.

Where can I find the fee?

Fees vary by session and category, and listings online disagree. Please confirm the current fee from the counselling notice or the college office.

Dates, eligibility and fees change every session. Always check the current notice from the Bihar School Examination Board before applying.
